Start with **Tutorial** in the game menu. It gives you a small, guided space to learn before you try a custom game against an AI.

## Give the colony a job

In Glob2, you manage work through buildings, flags, and areas. Select a building to adjust its assigned workers. The globs decide how to reach their jobs and when they need food or care.

Start with one change at a time: place a building, assign workers, and check whether workers arrive. If construction stalls, inspect the required resources, the available workers, and the route to the site.

## Keep food within reach

An inn feeds your globs, while a swarm produces more of them. Expansion adds mouths to feed. Before increasing production, make sure inns have wheat and that workers can reach the food supply.

Watch the colony’s hunger and health information. A larger colony is only useful if its food and services keep up.

## Try a quiet practice game

Choose **Custom Game**, pick a map, and add an AI opponent. Practice keeping an inn supplied, building a second service, and using an exploration flag. Map conditions and custom rules can change which opening works best.
