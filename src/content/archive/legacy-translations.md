The wiki’s manual index links to community translations. These remain valuable historical resources, though their age and completeness vary. They are not translations of our current guides.

- [Français](https://globulation2.org/wiki/Fr:User_Manual)
- [Deutsch](https://globulation2.org/wiki/De:User_Manual)
- [Español](https://globulation2.org/wiki/Es:User_Manual)

The [original manual index](https://globulation2.org/wiki/User_Manual) also links to Italian, Czech, Romanian, Russian, Catalan, Finnish, Japanese, and Chinese pages. Visit that index to reach those originals.

Would you like to help maintain a current translation? [Contribute through the game repository](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2). Please identify the language and the pages you want to work on so another speaker can review the result.
